Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Shelf to Sense Channel Manager accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google and YouTube data.

1. Purpose and scope

Shelf to Sense Channel Manager is a private tool operated by the owner of the Shelf to Sense YouTube channel. It is used only to manage the owner's authorized channel and is not offered to the general public.

2. Data the tool may access

After the channel owner grants OAuth permission, the tool may access:

  • YouTube channel identity and channel metadata;
  • Video titles, descriptions, tags, visibility, thumbnails, captions, and upload status;
  • Playlists, playlist items, comments, and moderation status;
  • YouTube Analytics metrics such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, and subscriber activity;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to perform owner-authorized operations.

The tool does not request the user's Google password.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to carry out instructions from the authorized channel owner, including uploading and managing videos, organizing channel content, assisting with comments, and producing private performance reports.

Google user data is not used for advertising, credit decisions, surveillance, or training public artificial-intelligence models.

4. Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on an owner-controlled computer with restricted file permissions. The tool does not operate a public credential server or public user database. OAuth credentials are never included in this public website or its source repository.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or transferred to data brokers. Data may be transmitted to Google and YouTube only as necessary to call their official APIs. Disclosure may occur only when required by applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

Local OAuth credentials are retained until the channel owner revokes access or deletes the local credential files. Operational data is retained only as long as needed for channel management and troubleshooting.

The channel owner can revoke access at any time from Google Account Connections. After revocation, local credentials can be deleted from the owner-controlled machine.

7. Google API Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the tool's functionality or legal requirements change. Questions or deletion requests can be submitted through the project's public support page. Do not include passwords, OAuth codes, or tokens in a support request.
